Frequently Asked Questions
‍Complete transparency

What are the costs for my venue?

Zero upfront costs, zero maintenance fees, zero refill charges. Your only expense is electricity - typically £5-10 per month. AIRO covers installation, maintenance, fragrances, payment processing, insurance, and all repairs. You provide the space and power outlet, we handle everything else.

How does the revenue split work?

Simple and fair: 50/50 net profit split paid monthly via bank transfer. We deduct the wholesale cost of fragrances and payment processing fees, then split the remaining profit equally. You receive detailed monthly statements showing all transactions, costs, and your payout. Average venue earns £200-800/month depending on foot traffic.

What happens if the machine breaks or gets damaged?

AIRO assumes 100% liability for repairs and maintenance. If something breaks - technical malfunction, vandalism, accident - we fix or replace it at no cost to you. Our remote monitoring alerts us to issues proactively. Average response time: same business day for technical issues, within 72 hours for on-site repairs.

Can the cost of fragrance  be changed?

We schedule a site visit to assess your venue and identify the optimal placement - typically in restroom areas for privacy and high usage. Our certified electrician handles the complete installation, ensuring all safety and electrical compliance. We configure the machine, load fragrances, train your staff, and test the system. Total process: 2-4 hours. You're earning that same day.

Who handles maintenance and refills?

AIRO handles everything. We monitor fragrance levels remotely and schedule refills before you run out. Regular maintenance visits every 4-6 weeks include cleaning, software updates, and quality checks. All fragrance refills, technical support, and repairs are our responsibility. Your staff does nothing except occasionally wipe down the exterior if desired.

What security features protect against theft or vandalism?

Our dispensers feature shatter-proof construction, integrated alarm systems, and secure mounting. Any tampering triggers an instant alert to both you and our team. The machine is bolted securely and requires specialized tools to remove. All fragrances are locked inside. We also carry comprehensive insurance covering theft, vandalism, and damage.

What designer fragrances do you offer?

We stock 5-8 premium designer brands per machine including YSL, Dior, Tom Ford, Armani, Versace, Paco Rabanne, and other luxury labels. Fragrance selection is customized based on your venue type and clientele demographics. We analyze usage data and adjust the lineup quarterly to maximize appeal and revenue. All authentic, all premium.

Where should the dispenser be placed?

Restrooms generate the highest usage - guests naturally think about fragrance there, and privacy encourages use. We recommend placement near sinks or mirrors in high-traffic restroom areas. Alternative locations include VIP lounges, coat check areas, or entrance/exit corridors. During our site visit, we'll identify the optimal spot based on your venue layout, foot traffic, and electrical access.

What about insurance and liability?

AIRO carries comprehensive insurance covering the equipment, inventory, liability, and any incidents related to the dispenser. You have zero liability for the machine or its operation. Our insurance covers theft, vandalism, malfunction, and any theoretical customer issues. We provide certificates of insurance upon request for your records or landlord requirements.
