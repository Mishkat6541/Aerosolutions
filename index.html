Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with AIRO

What are the operating costs for the machine?
The only operating cost is electricity, which is minimal (approximately £2-3 per month). AIRO handles all fragrance refills, maintenance, repairs, and monitoring at no cost to you. There are no upfront fees, monthly fees, or hidden charges.
What security features does the machine have?
Every AIRO dispenser includes an integrated alarm system with remote monitoring. If tampering is detected, we receive instant alerts and can take immediate action. The construction is shatter-proof and designed to withstand high-traffic environments. All units are also fully insured.
How much fragrance does each spray dispense?
Our default setting dispenses 0.13ml per spray, which is the perfect amount for a quality application without waste. This ensures optimal fragrance experience while maximizing value for both the user and your venue. Settings can be adjusted based on specific needs.
Can we adjust pricing or customize the fragrances?
Absolutely! We work with each venue to determine optimal pricing based on your clientele and market. Fragrance selection is also customizable - we'll curate a collection that aligns with your venue's brand and guest preferences. LED colors can also be customized to match your aesthetic.
Who is responsible for repairs and liability?
AIRO handles all maintenance, repairs, and technical issues. If something goes wrong, we fix it - at no cost to you. All machines are fully insured and we carry comprehensive liability coverage. Your venue has zero risk or responsibility for equipment issues.
How are fragrances selected and replaced?
We monitor usage data to track which fragrances are most popular and ensure optimal inventory. Refills are handled proactively based on usage patterns - you'll never run out. We work with you to adjust the fragrance lineup based on seasonal trends and customer feedback.
Where should the machine be placed for best results?
Ideal placements include restroom corridors, VIP lounge areas, or high-traffic areas near exits. We provide consultation on optimal placement based on your venue layout to maximize visibility and usage. The machine requires a standard power outlet and works well in most restroom or corridor environments.
Is the machine covered by insurance?
Yes, all AIRO dispensers are fully insured for theft, damage, and liability. We carry comprehensive coverage so your venue is completely protected. In the unlikely event of any incident, AIRO handles all claims and replacements.
